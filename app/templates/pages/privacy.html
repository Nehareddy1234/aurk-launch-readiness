YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Information used by the store

When you create an account, we store your name, email address and a password hash. When you place an order, we store the products, quantities, prices, order status, recipient name, phone number and delivery address. If you choose to save an address, it is also stored in your account for future checkout.

Optional sign-in and community features

If you add phone sign-in, we store that number as an account credential. If you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies a verified email address, name and account identifier. We use these for sign-in and do not request access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.

Photos submitted to Worn by you are reviewed before publication. Approved photos and the display name you supply are public. You can withdraw your photo through your account. Notify me records the product you requested and sends an acknowledgement and availability update.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide your account and bag, process and deliver orders, contact you about confirmation or delivery, handle returns and refunds, and protect the store against misuse. Order and payment records support order administration and record keeping.

Cookies and browser storage

Essential session cookies keep you signed in, remember a guest bag and protect forms. A guest bag stores product-option identifiers and quantities; prices are checked against our catalogue. Clearing cookies can remove your guest bag and sign you out. This store does not include advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts.

Payments and refund requests

Checkout currently uses cash on delivery and does not ask for card details. For a UPI or cash refund, our team coordinates the necessary details directly with you. Never send your password, UPI PIN or banking OTP to our support team.

Sharing for orders and support

Relevant delivery details are provided to the people arranging and fulfilling your order. Order details may be included in staff email or WhatsApp alerts when those services are enabled. If you contact us through email or WhatsApp, the service you choose also processes your messages under its own privacy terms.

Service providers

The store uses AWS for hosting, Brevo for transactional email, and DTDC for deliveries. The information needed to perform those services is processed by the relevant provider. Essential account and order updates are separate from advertising.

Keeping and protecting information

Account, order and support records are kept for store operations, support and applicable record-keeping obligations. Access to customer orders and administrative functions is restricted. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

To ask about your data, correct an error, request a copy or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact support@aurk.in. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you. Some order records may need to be retained for outstanding orders, disputes or legal obligations.

Contact and updates

You can also reach AURK at +91 9392218069. Updates to this notice will be published on this page with a revised date.
