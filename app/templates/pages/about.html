We didn’t start AURK because the world needed another clothing brand. It started with a question. Why does something have to look “Indian” to feel Indian? Our generation grew up differently. We carry where we come from, but we’re constantly moving forward. New cities. New ambitions. New versions of ourselves. And I wanted to build something that represented that. Not tradition printed on a T-shirt. Not another oversized tee with a graphic slapped on the back. Something deeper. A brand where every piece begins with an idea — a feeling, a story, a part of who we are. That became AURK. Built in India. Inspired by where we come from. Designed for where we’re going. We’re still at the beginning. The samples aren’t perfect yet. The first drop isn’t even in your hands. But maybe that’s exactly why I wanted to tell you this now. So when you eventually see AURK out there, you’ll know you were here before it became something. AURK.
