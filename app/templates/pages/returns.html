AFTER YOUR DELIVERY

Returns & exchanges.

You can request a return or exchange within 5 days after delivery.

Item condition

Items requested for return or exchange must be unused, unwashed, and have their original tags attached.

Return shipping

Customers are responsible for the cost of shipping items back for a return or exchange.

Refund methods

Refunds are issued through UPI or cash within 3–5 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. View refund information.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your item is damaged, defective, incorrect or does not match its description, contact us with your order number and details. These store policies do not limit your rights under applicable consumer law.

How to make a request

Contact AURK within the 5-day request window. Include your order number, the item, and whether you would like a return or an exchange.

For an exchange, tell us which size or colour you would prefer.
