SHOPPING WITH AURK

Terms of purchase.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Browsing and your account

You can browse and build a bag without signing in. An account is required at checkout so you can view your orders. Provide accurate contact and delivery details, keep your password private and contact us if you suspect unauthorised account use.

Products, prices and availability

Prices are shown in Indian rupees. The selected size or colour can have its own price, which is shown before adding it to your bag. Review the checkout total before placing your order. Adding an item to your bag does not reserve stock; availability is checked when your order is placed.

Order confirmation and payment

New orders await our team's confirmation. After confirmation, your order status changes and you will receive a call shortly. Cash on delivery is the current payment method: no payment is collected online when you place an order. Pay the order amount on delivery.

Delivery

Delivery is free on confirmed orders to serviceable addresses in India, with no minimum order value. Estimated delivery is 3–5 business days after confirmation. See our shipping information.

Changes and cancellations

Contact us with your order number if you need a change or cancellation. Our team can cancel an order before it is shipped. After shipment, contact us about the available return or exchange process.

Returns, exchanges and refunds

Request a return or exchange within 5 days after delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed and have their original tags attached. Customers pay the cost of sending items back. Refunds are issued by UPI or cash within 3–5 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Read the returns and exchanges and refund pages before requesting one.

Problems with an order

If an item is damaged, defective, incorrect or does not match its description, contact us with your order number and details of the problem. These store policies do not limit consumer rights or remedies available under applicable law.

Responsible use

Do not misuse accounts, submit fraudulent orders, attempt unauthorised access or interfere with the store. Contact us to report a technical problem.

Privacy and support

Our privacy policy explains how account and order information is used. For purchase questions, email support@aurk.in or contact +91 9392218069.
