How to measure

Body chest
Measure around the fullest part of your chest.
Flat chest
Measure your T-shirt across the chest, laid flat.
Length
Highest shoulder point to hem.
Shoulder
Shoulder seam to shoulder seam.
Sleeve
Shoulder seam to sleeve hem.

At a range boundary, choose the smaller size: 36 inches = S; above 36 to 38 inches = M. Choose an adjacent size for your preferred roominess.

T-shirt measurements are finished garment measurements before customer washing.
